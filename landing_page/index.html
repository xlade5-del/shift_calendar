Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How is VelloShift different from Google Calendar?

Google Calendar was built for meetings and appointments, not rotating shifts. VelloShift offers instant conflict detection, automatic partner syncing (<1 second), a free time finder, and works offline—features Google Calendar doesn't have for shift workers.

Does my partner need a separate subscription?

Nope! One person can be on Free and the other on Premium—you'll still sync perfectly. If you both want premium features (like unlimited iCal feeds), you'll each need a Premium subscription.

Can I import shifts from my employer's scheduling system?

Yes! If your employer provides an iCal feed (most hospital systems like Epic MyChart, When I Work, Deputy do), you can import it in one click. Free accounts get 1 feed, Premium gets unlimited.

What if we both work nights or have no internet at work?

VelloShift works completely offline. Add or update shifts anytime—they'll sync automatically when you're back online. No internet required to use the app.

Is my schedule data private and secure?

Absolutely. Your data is end-to-end encrypted, GDPR compliant, and only visible to you and your partner. We never sell or share your information. Read our Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes! Cancel anytime from Settings → Manage Subscription. You'll keep Premium features until the end of your billing period, then automatically revert to the Free plan—no data lost.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day free trial (no credit card required). If you subscribe and aren't satisfied within 30 days, email support@velloshift.com for a full refund—no questions asked.
